# Six generated French image proposals — pending review

Built-in image_gen outputs dated 30 October 2026. The twelve WebP files here are
whole-frame 512/256 reductions; they total 202,100 bytes. The six full-resolution
1254×1254 PNG source files (9,839,323 bytes) are included in `sources/` and
verified against their exact byte lengths and SHA-256 hashes in `manifest.json`.
Exact source filenames, prompts and derivative metadata remain in that manifest.
The unrelated seventh source was not selected, imported or altered.

These are proposals for pain/bain, poule/boule and poire/boire in fr-fr-pilot.
They carry unknown rights, no competent human approval and no child trial result.
The /pw/–/bw/ context of poire/boire remains separate from simple /p/–/b/ onset.
The held-out pomme/porte/poupée/papillon words are neither generated nor assigned
by this pack. No probe, reserve, programme or historical review is modified.

```text
python manage.py install_generated_review_pack --actor EXISTING_EDITOR --source-dir content\review_packs\fr-p-b-initial-review-20261030\sources
python manage.py install_generated_review_pack --actor EXISTING_EDITOR --source-dir content\review_packs\fr-p-b-initial-review-20261030\sources --apply
```

The first command is read-only. Apply requires an active editor of the exact
French variety and all three existing real candidate series. It creates eighteen
private MediaAssets (six quality sources, twelve display variants), three new real
draft versions and an immutable import receipt. It creates no account, evidence,
review, availability decision, publication or family assignment. Repeating the
same named exact manifest returns the receipt, without new files or versions.
Changed source/variant bytes or a changed named manifest cannot silently replace
an imported item. A later substantial revision needs a new pack/item version.

Media URLs returned by the command use authenticated protected delivery. The
uploader and editors/reviewers scoped to the exact variety can inspect the source
and derivatives. Family and unrelated accounts cannot access these draft media.
`editorial_image_srcset` supplies protected 256w/512w URLs for responsive display.
The activity refers to the 512 asset; full-quality source and both derivatives
remain linked through the receipt and draft item metadata.

Technical visual review observed whole subjects, a common light background and
no written cues at 256 px. This is not clinical, native-speaker or child testing.
Mandatory review flags: bath may be named baignoire; sphere may be named balle;
the drinking person's glass is small at 120 px and may require a revised version
after recognition trials. Never show a word label or play the target model before
the spontaneous naming observation.

Use [the review and naming protocol](../../../docs/GENERATED_IMAGE_REVIEW_PACK_20261030.md),
`recognition-naming-template.csv` and `pieces-responsables.csv`. Keep the pack at
